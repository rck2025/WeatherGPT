Frequently Asked Questions

How does WeatherGPT generate alerts?+
WeatherGPT aggregates real-time data from IMD, NDMA, and regional meteorological stations, then uses AI to analyse patterns and generate severity-ranked alerts in plain language across multiple Indian languages.
What languages are supported?+
The AI Query Bot supports auto-detection across major Indian languages including Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, Telugu, Marathi, and English. Responses are generated in the detected language automatically.
How often is weather data updated?+
Core weather metrics refresh every 10 minutes. Hazard overlays and severe weather alerts update in near real-time as new bulletins are issued by IMD and NDMA.
What is the difference between GPS and Manual location mode?+
GPS mode automatically detects your current location for hyper-local weather data. Manual mode lets you enter any city name, district, or coordinates to get weather information for a specific area.
Who provides the underlying weather data?+
Data is sourced from the India Meteorological Department (IMD), National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA), and Open-Meteo for extended global coverage and historical analysis.
Is WeatherGPT free to use?+
Yes — WeatherGPT is a public service provided under the Ministry of Earth Sciences meteorological intelligence network. All alerts and forecasts are freely accessible to citizens.
